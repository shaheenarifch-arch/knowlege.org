# Community and Religious-Safety Guardrails

## Objective
Share well-sourced Qur'anic knowledge globally and help people explore constructive changes in character, reflection, responsibility and service. Avoid coercion and never promise guaranteed personal or spiritual outcomes.

## Every submission passes
1. Input classifier.
2. Source-integrity check.
3. Citation-coverage check.
4. Sectarian/divisive-language check.
5. Personal-ruling check.
6. Output checker.
7. Human moderation when any check returns REVIEW.
8. Audit record.

## Automatically blocked
- Invented verses, hadith, grading, quotations or consensus.
- Sectarian superiority, takfir or declarations about another person's faith.
- Incitement, harassment or demeaning religious communities.
- Political campaigning presented as binding religion.
- Personal fatwas generated by the portal.

## Human review
- Jurisprudential disputes.
- Personal marriage, divorce, inheritance and financial questions.
- Political questions, war, conflict or public accusations.
- Content about abuse, crisis, health or legal issues.

## Allowed educational scope
- Reviewed Qur'anic themes.
- Clearly attributed translation and tafsir comparison.
- Historical and scholarly context.
- Character, mercy, justice, dignity, family, responsibility, knowledge and service.
- Neutral summaries of documented differences with citations.
