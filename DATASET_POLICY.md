# Dataset policy

1. Obtain datasets from authorized or public-domain sources.
2. Record the canonical source URL, version, edition, license, attribution and checksum.
3. Do not import a manifest unless `review.status` is `APPROVED`.
4. Keep Qur'anic Arabic, translation, tafsir, hadith, scholar biography and editorial explanation as separate content types.
5. Never infer or invent hadith grading.
6. Do not expose imported content publicly until a human reviewer approves the records.
7. Track corrections and superseded dataset versions rather than silently overwriting history.
