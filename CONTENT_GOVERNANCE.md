# Content Governance
- Keep Qur'anic Arabic immutable and separate from translation and commentary.
- Show translator, author, edition, license, reviewer and last-reviewed date.
- Never invent hadith grading or scholarly consensus.
- AI may summarize retrieved sources with citations but must not issue personal religious rulings.
- Charity beneficiaries must not be exposed or used for manipulative storytelling.
- Help resources require jurisdiction, eligibility, official URL and review date.
